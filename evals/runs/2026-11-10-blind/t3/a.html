How it works

Three choices, then it runs itself.

Set it up in a couple of minutes. Change it whenever your coffee habits do.

  1. Pick your roast

    Light and fruity, balanced and chocolatey, or dark and bold. Not sure? Take the discovery bag and we’ll rotate.

  2. Set your rhythm

    Every week, every two weeks or every month. Tell us whether you grind at home, and we’ll grind for you if you don’t.

  3. Open the box

    Beans are roasted after you order and arrive in a letterbox-friendly bag. Skip a delivery or pause from your account.

Questions

Things people ask first.

How fresh will my beans be?

Roasted after you order and posted within 48 hours. Beans taste their best in the first few weeks after roasting, and we print the date on each bag so you can tell.

Can I change roast or size later?

Yes. Update your next delivery any time before it’s roasted, and the change carries forward.

I don’t own a grinder. Can you grind it?

Yes. Choose espresso, filter or French press grind at sign-up and we’ll set it up for you.

What if I’m going away?

Pause your subscription from your account and restart when you’re home.
