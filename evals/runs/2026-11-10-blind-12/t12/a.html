Getting there

Free shuttle from Riverbend Station every 15 minutes. Bike valet at the north gate.

Bring

Blankets and low chairs are welcome. Sealed water bottles are fine; no glass or outside alcohol.

Food

Twelve local food trucks, a farmers' market tent, and vegetarian options at every stall.

Access

Step-free paths, accessible viewing platforms and quiet area. Contact us for assistance.
