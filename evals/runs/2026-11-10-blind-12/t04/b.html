Our little studio

Twelve mats. One warm room. Everyone knows your name.

Larch Lane is a small space on purpose. Classes are capped at twelve, so your teacher can see you, adjust you gently, and notice when you come back.

We are not chasing the perfect pose. We are here for a calmer week, easier shoulders, and a quiet hour that belongs to you.
