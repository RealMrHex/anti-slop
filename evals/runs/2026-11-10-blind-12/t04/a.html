A studio for the street, not the scene

Fernhill began in 2016 when Maya started teaching in the back room of the community hall. Neighbours kept turning up, so we found a proper space with wooden floors and a window that catches the morning.

We keep it small on purpose. You will see the same faces, get real adjustments, and leave feeling a bit better than you arrived.
