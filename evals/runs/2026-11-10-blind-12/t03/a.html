Burst Tomato Rigatoni with Burrata

Cherry tomatoes blister in olive oil until they collapse into a sweet, garlicky sauce. Finish with cool, creamy burrata.

★★★★★4.8 · 312 ratings
Time
35 min
Level
Easy
Per serving
610 kcal

Ingredients

4 servings

    Method

    1. Boil the pasta water

      Salt a large pot generously. Cook the rigatoni 1 minute short of the packet time and save a mug of the cooking water.

      10 min

    2. Blister the tomatoes

      Warm the oil with garlic and chili flakes over medium heat. Add tomatoes and a pinch of salt; cover and cook until they start to pop.

      8 min

    3. Crush into a sauce

      Press the tomatoes with the back of a spoon so they release their juices. Simmer uncovered until glossy and jammy.

      6 min

    4. Toss and finish

      Add the pasta and a splash of cooking water. Toss over the heat until the sauce clings. Tear the burrata on top and scatter with basil.

    Cook's tip

    Pull the burrata from the fridge 20 minutes before serving. Cold cheese on hot pasta stiffens up; room temperature stays soft and creamy.
