Creamy Tomato Basil Pasta

A weeknight comfort dish with roasted tomatoes and fresh basil. ★ 4.8 (312 reviews)

10 minPrep
20 minCook
EasyLevel

Ingredients 4 servings

    Method

    1. 1

      Boil the pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve a cup of the pasta water, then drain.
      About 10 min

    2. 2

      Sauté garlic in olive oil for 30 seconds, add tomatoes and simmer until they break down.
      About 8 min

    3. 3

      Stir in cream and a splash of pasta water. Toss with the pasta until glossy.

    4. 4

      Finish with torn basil, parmesan and black pepper. Serve immediately.
