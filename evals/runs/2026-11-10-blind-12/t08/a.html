Selected work

Five recent frames from ongoing series. Placeholder artwork stands in until the real photographs are dropped in.

I wait, then I press the shutter once.

Most of my best frames come from standing in the same place far longer than is reasonable.

I started on my father's secondhand Pentax and never really stopped. Today I split my year between commissioned editorial and portrait work and a long-running series on the Swedish west coast.

Clients usually hear from me within two working days. I shoot to a brief but leave room for whatever the light decides to do.
