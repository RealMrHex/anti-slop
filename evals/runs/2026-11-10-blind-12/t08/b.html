About

I have spent twelve years learning to wait: for the cloud to move, the shoulders to drop, the room to forget I am in it.

My work began in local newsrooms and grew into long-form documentary projects and commissioned editorial. I shoot mostly natural light, on film and digital, and deliver edited, colour-consistent sets you can use right away.
