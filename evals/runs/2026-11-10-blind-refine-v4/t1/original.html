Can I choose my plants?+

Yes. We suggest matches from your quiz, and you can swap or skip any month before it ships.

What if a plant dies?+

Send us a photo within 60 days and we will replace it, no charge, and help figure out what went wrong.

Are the plants safe for pets?+

Tell us about your pets in the quiz and we will only send pet-friendly varieties.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Always. Pause, skip, or cancel from your account in two clicks. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata.
